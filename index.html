Our Story

Built local. Detailed by hand. No shortcuts.

J&T Mobile Detailing started right here in Starkville with one simple idea: your vehicle deserves a real, hands-on detail without you ever leaving home. What began as two college students detailing cars around their class schedule has grown into a full mobile crew — we load up the truck and come to you, whether that's a driveway in town or a barn out in the country.

We treat every truck, car, trailer and UTV like it's our own — because around here, your rig is part of the family too.
